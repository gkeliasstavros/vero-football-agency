This page explains how the VERO website and email enquiry route work.

Who handles enquiries

VERO Football Agency is operated by a company registered in Bulgaria. Business correspondence address: Boulevard Vitosha 63, Sofia, Bulgaria. For questions about an enquiry or your personal information, email verofootballagency@gmail.com.

What happens on this website

When you submit the enquiry form, the website sends the details you entered to VERO by email through the hosting server. It does not save the enquiry to a website database. A local rate limit records a one-way identifier for your network address and recent submission times for up to one hour to reduce spam. If the server cannot accept the message, you can choose to open your email app or copy the prepared message on your device.

Enquiries and email

A form submission or email may include your name, email address, role, organisation and whatever details you write. VERO uses that information to read and respond to your request, discuss a possible working relationship and keep relevant correspondence. Your email provider and VERO's email provider process the message to deliver and store it. Avoid including sensitive personal information in an initial enquiry unless it is necessary.

Purpose, legal basis and retention

We use enquiry correspondence to respond to you and, where relevant, take steps you request toward a possible professional relationship. Depending on your request, this is based on steps before entering into a contract at your request or our legitimate interest in handling professional enquiries. We use the temporary anti-spam record to protect the contact service from misuse. We keep website enquiry correspondence for one year after the last message about the enquiry, unless a legal obligation requires a longer period. You can ask about a particular message or request its deletion using the email above.

Website services

This site loads web fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive technical request information such as your IP address. Hostinger serves the website and may process routine server logs. We do not use website analytics or advertising trackers.

Your choices and rights

You can leave the form without submitting it. If you send an enquiry, you can contact VERO to ask about access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection where applicable. You may also contact your local data protection authority. The exact scope of these rights depends on the circumstances.

Changes

This information describes the website as published on 26 September 2026. It will need updating if analytics or other services are added.
