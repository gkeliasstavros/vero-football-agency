# VERO player research — 3 October 2026

31 represented players. Current representation, clubs and playing positions come from the agency’s confirmed roster; external records enrich the profiles. Four players are confirmed free agents. Marina Theodoraki is at Medyk Konin on loan from GKS Katowice.

## Method and publication rules

Official club and federation records take priority for biographical and career information. Soccerdonna and Sofascore supplement those records, particularly for dates and selected career entries. Searches included native-language spellings. Unrelated namesakes, team photographs without an identified individual, generic silhouettes and default database images were excluded. Exact contract ends, market values, medical information, personal contact details and unsupported statistics were not added.

Career tables show selected entries, not a complete transfer audit. “Previously” means the club is documented but a precise period was not established. An international selection camp is described as a camp, not as a senior cap. Current club and positional conflicts in external databases are resolved using VERO’s explicit confirmation. Statistics on Irina Topal and Valeriia Postol are snapshots of the club’s 2026 Moterų A lyga records, checked on 3 October 2026; they do not update automatically.

## Photo provenance

The four original photographs supplied by VERO are retained. Twenty-three additional public club, federation, league or player-record photographs were visually reviewed, attributed and saved locally. Public availability does not establish reuse permission; no licence clearance is claimed. VERO should confirm publication rights with the player, club or photographer. Source URLs and status are recorded in players.json. Website delivery copies preserve aspect ratio and the complete image, with no generated faces or retouching. Uniform 4:5 frames use contain rather than cropping. Some club announcements are graphics and some federation images are low resolution; those can be upgraded with agency originals.

## Conflicts and decisions

- Several databases display old clubs; the confirmed agency roster controls current club status.
- LASK’s official 7 July 2026 announcement says Katrin Kirpu arrived from ŽNK Agram. Finnish league records also document Åland United in 2024–25. Both are preserved as selected career entries.
- The Olympiacos Anna-Maria Panagiotopoulou announcement is indexed with her 30 May 2003 birth date and 2017–22 Mesolongiou / 2022–26 Trikala history. Its URL currently returns 404, so the public profile links to the corroborating EPO record instead of a dead announcement.
- Some club/federation sources classify Antria Efstratiou, Bojana Petkova, Asimina Chatziioannou and others differently. VERO’s confirmed playing roles are displayed.
- The unrelated Despoina Rokoma marathon/academic records and adult Georgia Michou professional profiles were excluded.
